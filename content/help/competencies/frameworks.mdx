# Setting Up Competency Frameworks

<RoleTag role="hr">HR</RoleTag> <RoleTag role="admin">Admin</RoleTag>

Competency frameworks define the skills and behaviors your organization values. They serve as the foundation for review questions, calibration heatmaps, and career-level expectations.

## Importing System Frameworks

Perf includes four pre-built frameworks you can import with one click:

| Framework | Best For |
|---|---|
| **Engineering Ladder** | Software engineering IC and staff tracks |
| **Product Management** | Product managers and product leaders |
| **People Management** | Management roles across departments |
| **General (SHRM-style)** | Universal competencies for any function |

Navigate to **Dashboard > Templates**, click **Import Framework**, select one, and click **Import**.

<Callout type="info">
Imported frameworks are fully editable after import without affecting the original system template.
</Callout>

## Creating Custom Competencies

Click **Add Competency** and fill in a **Name**, **Description**, **Category** (Core, Technical, Leadership, Communication, Collaboration, or Problem Solving), and the **Core** toggle if it applies to every employee.

## Mapping Competencies to Levels

Switch to the **Matrix** view. Rows are competencies, columns are career levels. Click any cell to set expected proficiency (1-5):

| Proficiency | Label | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Basic | Aware of the concept; needs guidance |
| 2 | Developing | Can apply with some support |
| 3 | Proficient | Independently competent |
| 4 | Advanced | Coaches others; handles complex scenarios |
| 5 | Expert | Recognized authority; shapes org-wide standards |

<Callout type="tip">
Leave cells empty for competencies not relevant at a given level. For example, "Organizational Strategy" may only apply at senior and staff levels.
</Callout>

## Adding Behavioral Indicators

For each proficiency level, define observable behaviors that describe what that rating looks like in practice. Indicators make reviews more objective by giving reviewers a concrete reference point.

## How Competencies Feed into Reviews

- **Cycle creation** -- select which competencies to include in the review template
- **Nami DMs** -- reviewers rate each competency with its description shown for context
- **Calibration heatmaps** -- competency ratings visualized across departments and levels
- **Analytics** -- track competency trends over multiple cycles

<Callout type="warning">
Changing competency names or deleting competencies after a cycle has launched will not affect in-flight reviews. Historical data is preserved under the original name.
</Callout>
