# How to Complete a Self-Review

<RoleTag role="all">All users</RoleTag>

Self-reviews are completed entirely through Slack by chatting with **Nami**, the Perf AI assistant. There is no need to open a separate app or fill out a form -- everything happens in your Slack DMs.

## Receiving Your Review Assignment

When a performance cycle launches that includes you, Nami sends you a direct message in Slack. The message includes:

- The name of the cycle (e.g., "Q1 2026 Performance Review")
- The deadline for completion
- A **"Let's go"** button to start

<Callout type="info">
You will only receive a self-review DM if your admin or HR team has included you in the active cycle and selected self-review as one of the review types.
</Callout>

## Starting the Review

Click the **"Let's go"** button in Nami's message. Nami walks you through each question one at a time in a conversational format.

### Rating Questions (1-5 Scale)

For competency-based questions, you select a rating using interactive buttons:

| Rating | Label | Meaning |
|--------|-------|---------|
| 1 | Needs Development | Significant gaps; requires focused improvement |
| 2 | Developing | Partially meets expectations; growing in this area |
| 3 | Meets Expectations | Consistently delivers at the expected level |
| 4 | Exceeds Expectations | Frequently goes above what is expected |
| 5 | Outstanding | Exceptional performance; a role model in this area |

### Text Questions

For open-ended questions (such as accomplishments, areas for growth, or feedback), simply type your response directly in the Slack chat. There is no character limit, but aim for thoughtful, specific answers.

<Callout type="tip">
Be specific in text responses. Instead of "I did well this quarter," describe concrete projects, outcomes, and impact. Your self-review provides context that your manager reads before writing their assessment.
</Callout>

## Using "Remind Me Later"

Not ready to complete the review right now? Click the **"Remind me later"** button and Nami will message you again after a delay. You can postpone multiple times, but keep the deadline in mind.

<Callout type="warning">
If you do not complete your self-review before the cycle deadline, it will be marked as incomplete. Your manager may still proceed with their review, but they will not have your self-assessment as context.
</Callout>

## After Submission

Once you answer all questions, Nami confirms your submission. Your responses are:

- Visible to your manager when they write their review
- Stored in your review history on the Perf dashboard
- Included in calibration sessions if your organization uses them

You cannot edit responses after submission. If you made a significant error, contact your HR admin to discuss options.
