# Kudos (Continuous Feedback)

<RoleTag role="all" />

Kudos in Nami lets you share recognition and constructive input with colleagues at any time, not just during formal review cycles. Use the `/kudos` slash command in Slack to send kudos instantly.

## What Are Kudos?

Unlike cycle-based reviews, kudos are ongoing and informal. They capture real-time observations so nothing is forgotten when review season arrives. All kudos are stored in the recipient's profile and can be referenced during formal assessments.

## Kudos Types

| Type | When to use |
|---|---|
| **Praise** | Recognize a job well done or a positive behavior |
| **Constructive** | Suggest a specific improvement with actionable advice |
| **General** | Share an observation that does not fit neatly into praise or constructive |

## Sending Kudos via Slack

Type `/kudos` in any Slack channel to open the kudos form. Fill in:

1. **Recipient** -- select the teammate you want to send kudos to.
2. **Feedback Type** -- choose Praise, Constructive, or General.
3. **Your message** -- write your kudos.
4. **Privacy options:**
   - **Send anonymously** -- hides your identity from everyone except HR/Admin.
   - **Share with recipient** (checked by default) -- controls whether the recipient can see this kudos. Uncheck to send private kudos visible only to the recipient's manager and HR.

## Visibility Rules

| Who | What they see |
|---|---|
| **Sender** | Always sees their own sent kudos |
| **Recipient** | Only sees kudos where "Share with recipient" was checked |
| **Recipient's manager** | Always sees all kudos for their direct reports |
| **HR / Admin** | Always sees all kudos across the workspace |

<Callout type="info">
Anonymous kudos hide your identity from the recipient and their manager. The `from_user` field is not stored in the database, so anonymity cannot be reversed -- even by admins.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
When "Share with recipient" is unchecked, the recipient will not receive a Slack notification and will not see the kudos on their dashboard. This is useful for constructive feedback intended only for the manager.
</Callout>

## How Recipients See Kudos

If "Share with recipient" is checked, the recipient receives a Slack DM from Nami with the kudos content and type. They can also view all received kudos on their **Dashboard > Kudos** page, organized by date and type.

<Callout type="tip">
Make kudos specific and timely. "Great job on the Q3 launch presentation -- your data storytelling really landed with the board" is far more useful than "Nice work."
</Callout>

Managers can see all kudos given to their direct reports (regardless of the "Share with recipient" setting), helping them build a fuller picture ahead of formal reviews.
