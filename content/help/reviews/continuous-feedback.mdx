# Continuous Feedback

<RoleTag role="all" />

Continuous feedback in Perf lets you share recognition and constructive input with colleagues at any time, not just during formal review cycles.

## What Is Continuous Feedback?

Unlike cycle-based reviews, continuous feedback is ongoing and informal. It captures real-time observations so nothing is forgotten when review season arrives. All feedback is stored in the recipient's profile and can be referenced during formal assessments.

## Feedback Types

| Type | When to use |
|---|---|
| **Praise** | Recognize a job well done or a positive behavior |
| **Constructive** | Suggest a specific improvement with actionable advice |
| **General** | Share an observation that does not fit neatly into praise or constructive |
| **Improvement** | Flag a recurring pattern that needs attention |
| **Question** | Ask for clarification or context about someone's work |

## Sending Feedback via Slack

1. Open a DM with **Nami** in Slack.
2. Tell Nami you want to give feedback, or use the shortcut from the Perf dashboard.
3. Select the recipient, choose a type, and write your message.
4. Optionally mark the feedback as **anonymous**.

<Callout type="info">
Anonymous feedback hides your identity from the recipient and their manager. Admins cannot reveal the sender either.
</Callout>

## How Recipients See Feedback

Recipients receive a Slack DM from Nami with the feedback content and type. They can also view all received feedback on their **Dashboard > Feedback** page, organized by date and type.

<Callout type="tip">
Make feedback specific and timely. "Great job on the Q3 launch presentation -- your data storytelling really landed with the board" is far more useful than "Nice work."
</Callout>

Managers can see feedback given to their direct reports, helping them build a fuller picture ahead of formal reviews.
