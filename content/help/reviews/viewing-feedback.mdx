# Viewing Your Performance & Kudos

<RoleTag role="all">Everyone</RoleTag>

The **Kudos** page in the Nami dashboard is your single destination for everything people have said about your work — from formal review responses to informal kudos sent via `/kudos`.

## Review Responses

Ratings and comments collected during formal review cycles. Each card shows:

- **Reviewer role** — Self, Manager, Peer, or Upward
- **Rating score** — numerical rating on the 1-5 scale
- **Written comments** — qualitative feedback for each question
- **Cycle name and date**

## Kudos

Ad-hoc feedback sent outside of formal cycles via the `/kudos` slash command, tagged with one of three types:

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **Praise** | Recognition for a job well done |
| **Constructive** | Suggestions for improvement |
| **General** | Observations that do not fit the other categories |

## Filtering and Searching

Use the filter bar to narrow results:

- **Source** — filter by Review Ratings or Kudos
- **Type** — filter kudos by Praise, Constructive, or General
- **Search** — free-text search across all comments

<Callout type="tip">
Combine filters to quickly find what you need. For example, select "Manager" source and search for "communication" to surface all related manager comments.
</Callout>

## Understanding Rating Scores

| Score | Label |
|---|---|
| 1 | Below expectations |
| 2 | Developing |
| 3 | Meets expectations |
| 4 | Exceeds expectations |
| 5 | Outstanding |

## Anonymous Feedback Indicator

Some feedback displays an **anonymous** badge. This applies to upward feedback (always anonymized), anonymous kudos, and peer feedback when the cycle is configured for anonymous peers.

## Who Can See What

| Role | Visibility |
|---|---|
| **Employee** | Own sent kudos + received kudos (only if sender chose "Share with recipient") |
| **Manager** | Own kudos plus all kudos for direct reports |
| **HR / Admin** | All kudos across the workspace |

<Callout type="info">
Managers can view direct reports' kudos regardless of the "Share with recipient" setting. However, they cannot see individual upward review responses about themselves — only the aggregated summary.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
If you see no kudos, try sending one via `/kudos` in Slack. For review responses, confirm that a review cycle has been completed and your assignments were submitted.
</Callout>
