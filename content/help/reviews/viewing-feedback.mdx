# Viewing Your Performance & Feedback

<RoleTag role="all">Everyone</RoleTag>

The **Feedback** page in the Perf dashboard is your single destination for everything people have said about your work — from formal review responses to informal continuous feedback.

## Review Responses

Ratings and comments collected during formal review cycles. Each card shows:

- **Reviewer role** — Self, Manager, Peer, or Upward
- **Rating score** — numerical rating on the 1-5 scale
- **Written comments** — qualitative feedback for each question
- **Cycle name and date**

## Continuous Feedback

Ad-hoc feedback sent outside of formal cycles, tagged with one of three types:

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **Praise** | Recognition for a job well done |
| **Constructive** | Suggestions for improvement |
| **General** | Observations that do not fit the other categories |

## Filtering and Searching

Use the filter bar to narrow results:

- **Source** — filter by reviewer role (Self, Manager, Peer, Upward)
- **Type** — filter continuous feedback by Praise, Constructive, or General
- **Search** — free-text search across all comments

<Callout type="tip">
Combine filters to quickly find what you need. For example, select "Manager" source and search for "communication" to surface all related manager comments.
</Callout>

## Understanding Rating Scores

| Score | Label |
|---|---|
| 1 | Needs improvement |
| 2 | Below expectations |
| 3 | Meets expectations |
| 4 | Exceeds expectations |
| 5 | Exceptional |

## Anonymous Feedback Indicator

Some feedback displays an **anonymous** badge. This applies to upward feedback (always anonymized) and peer feedback when the cycle is configured for anonymous peers.

## Who Can See What

| Role | Visibility |
|---|---|
| **Employee** | Own review responses and feedback received |
| **Manager** | Own feedback plus all feedback for direct reports |
| **HR / Admin** | All feedback across the workspace |

<Callout type="info">
Managers can view direct reports' feedback but cannot see individual upward responses about themselves — only the aggregated summary.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
If you see no feedback, confirm that a review cycle has been completed and your assignments were submitted. Draft responses do not appear until the reviewer submits them.
</Callout>
