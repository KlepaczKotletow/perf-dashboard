# Billing & Plans

<RoleTag role="admin" />

Perf offers four pricing tiers so you can start free and scale as your organization grows. All billing is handled through Stripe.

## Plan Comparison

| Feature | Free | Starter | Professional | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Price** | $0 | $5/user/mo | $10/user/mo | Custom |
| **Max users** | 5 | 50 | 500 | Unlimited |
| **Review cycles** | 1 active | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| **Survey types** | Pulse only | All | All | All |
| **Competency frameworks** | 1 | 3 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| **Analytics** | Basic | Full | Full + export | Full + API |
| **Calibration** | -- | -- | Included | Included |
| **Dedicated support** | -- | -- | -- | Included |

## How to Upgrade

1. Go to **Dashboard > Settings > Billing**.
2. Select the plan that fits your needs.
3. Enter payment details via the Stripe checkout flow.
4. Your workspace upgrades immediately and all features become available.

<Callout type="info">
Plan changes are prorated. If you upgrade mid-month, you only pay the difference for the remaining days.
</Callout>

## Managing Your Subscription

From the Billing page you can:

- View your current plan and usage.
- Update your payment method.
- Download invoices.
- Cancel or downgrade (takes effect at end of billing period).

<Callout type="warning">
Downgrading may disable features you are currently using. For example, moving from Professional to Starter removes calibration access. Review your usage before downgrading.
</Callout>

## Enterprise Plans

For organizations over 500 users or with advanced requirements (SSO, custom integrations, SLAs), contact sales for a tailored Enterprise agreement.
