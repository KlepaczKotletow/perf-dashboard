# Billing & Plans

<RoleTag role="admin" />

## How to Upgrade

1. Go to **Dashboard → Settings → Billing**.
2. Select the plan that fits your needs.
3. Complete the Stripe checkout flow with your payment details.
4. Your workspace upgrades immediately and all new features become available.

<Callout type="info">
Your workspace upgrades immediately upon successful payment. Billing details and invoices are managed through the Stripe customer portal.
</Callout>

## Managing Your Subscription

From the **Settings → Billing** page you can:

- View your current plan and active user count
- Open the **Stripe Customer Portal** to update payment methods
- Download invoices and payment history
- Cancel or downgrade your subscription (takes effect at the end of the current billing period)

<Callout type="warning">
Downgrading may disable features you are currently using. For example, moving from Professional to Starter removes calibration and custom template access. Review your active usage before downgrading.
</Callout>

## Free Plan

The free plan allows up to **5 users** with no credit card required. It includes one active review cycle, the Slack integration, and the competency framework — perfect for trying Perf before committing.

## Enterprise Plans

For organizations with over 500 users or advanced requirements (SSO/SAML, custom integrations, dedicated support, SLA guarantees), contact [sales@getperf.com](mailto:sales@getperf.com) for a tailored Enterprise agreement.

## Refund Policy

All subscription fees are non-refundable except where required by applicable law. If you believe a refund is warranted, contact [support@getperf.com](mailto:support@getperf.com) — requests are reviewed on a case-by-case basis.

## Cancellation

You can cancel your subscription at any time. After cancellation:

1. You retain full access until the end of your current billing period
2. Your data is retained for **30 days** to allow you to export
3. After 30 days, your workspace data is permanently deleted

To cancel, go to **Settings → Billing** and click **Manage Subscription** to open the Stripe portal.
