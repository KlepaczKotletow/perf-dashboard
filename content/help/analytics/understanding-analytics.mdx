# Understanding Analytics

<RoleTag role="hr" /> <RoleTag role="admin" /> <RoleTag role="manager" />

The Analytics module turns raw review data into actionable insights. Use it to spot trends, identify skill gaps, and measure the health of your performance culture.

## Available Views

### Review Trends
A line chart showing average ratings over time, broken down by cycle. Use this to see whether performance is improving, stable, or declining across review periods.

### Rating Distribution
A histogram of ratings (1-5) for any selected cycle. Healthy distributions typically show a bell curve; heavy skew toward 5s may indicate rating inflation.

### Competency Heatmaps
A color-coded grid displaying average competency scores by department or function. Darker cells indicate higher proficiency; lighter cells highlight gaps that may need learning and development investment.

### Demographics
Break down ratings by department, job family, career level, or tenure. This helps HR teams monitor equity and consistency across groups.

### Completion Rates
Track what percentage of reviews have been submitted on time versus overdue. Low completion rates may signal process friction or poor communication.

<Callout type="tip">
Combine filters to drill into specific slices -- for example, "Q4 2025 cycle + Engineering department + Senior level" to see how senior engineers performed last quarter.
</Callout>

## Filtering Data

Every analytics view supports filters for:

- **Cycle** -- select one or more review cycles.
- **Function** -- filter by job family or function.
- **Department** -- narrow to a specific department.

<Callout type="info">
Managers see analytics scoped to their direct reports only. HR and admins see organization-wide data.
</Callout>

## Exporting

Charts and underlying data can be exported for use in presentations or external BI tools.
