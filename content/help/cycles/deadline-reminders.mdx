# Deadline Reminders & Overdue Reviews

<RoleTag role="all" />

Perf uses Nami, your AI assistant in Slack, to keep everyone on track with review deadlines. Here is how the reminder system works.

## Automatic Reminder Schedule

Nami sends Slack DMs at three intervals before each phase deadline:

| When | Message |
|---|---|
| **7 days before** | Friendly heads-up that a deadline is approaching |
| **3 days before** | Reminder with a direct link to the pending review |
| **1 day before** | Urgent nudge highlighting the deadline is tomorrow |

Reminders are sent automatically -- no action needed from admins or HR.

<Callout type="info">
Reminders only go to participants who have not yet submitted. If you complete your review early, you will not receive further reminders for that phase.
</Callout>

## "Remind Me Later"

When Nami sends a reminder, you can tap **Remind me later**. This snoozes the notification and Nami will follow up again in a few hours, giving you time to finish other work first.

<Callout type="tip">
Use "Remind me later" sparingly. It is better to complete your review in one sitting while context is fresh.
</Callout>

## What Happens If You Miss a Deadline

If a phase deadline passes and your review is not submitted:

1. Your review status changes to **Overdue**.
2. Nami sends a final notification letting you know you are past due.
3. Your manager and HR can see the overdue status on their dashboards.
4. You can still submit -- the review is not locked -- but the late submission is recorded.

<Callout type="warning">
Consistently overdue reviews may delay the entire cycle from progressing to the next phase. Submit on time to keep the process moving for everyone.
</Callout>
