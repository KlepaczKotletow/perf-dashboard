# Creating & Launching a Performance Cycle

<RoleTag role="admin">Admin</RoleTag> <RoleTag role="hr">HR</RoleTag>

Performance cycles are the core unit of review management in Nami. Use the 5-step creation wizard to configure everything from participants to questions before launching.

## Cycle Types

Before creating a cycle, choose the type that matches your review cadence:

| Type | Typical Duration | Use Case |
|------|-----------------|----------|
| Annual | 12 months | Comprehensive year-end review |
| Mid-Year | 6 months | Check-in at the halfway point |
| Quarterly | 3 months | Frequent, lightweight reviews |
| Probation | 1-6 months | New hire evaluation period |
| Custom | Any duration | Ad-hoc or project-based reviews |

## The 5-Step Wizard

Navigate to **Dashboard > Cycles** and click **Create Cycle** to open the wizard.

### Step 1: Basics & Dates

Set the cycle name, type, and key dates:

- **Cycle name** -- e.g., "Q1 2026 Performance Review"
- **Start date** -- when the review period begins
- **End date** -- when the review period ends
- **Review deadline** (optional) -- when all reviews must be completed

### Step 2: People

Select who participates. You can include people by:

- Department (e.g., all of Engineering)
- Individual selection
- Entire workspace

Nami automatically generates self-reviews, manager reviews, and upward reviews based on the selected participants and their manager relationships.

### Step 3: Questions

Select competencies to include in the review and add text questions. You can optionally apply a template from the template library. Templates include pre-built competency frameworks. You can mix rating questions (1-5 scale) with open-ended text questions.

<Callout type="tip">
Start with a built-in template for your first cycle. You can duplicate and customize it later once you understand how questions flow through Nami.
</Callout>

### Step 4: Nami Bot Configuration

Configure how Nami delivers reviews in Slack:

- **Send immediately or schedule** -- deliver all DMs on launch, or schedule for a specific date
- **Skip Nami** -- optionally disable Nami DMs if you prefer dashboard-only reviews
- **DM preview** -- see how many DMs will be sent to participants

### Step 5: Review & Launch

Preview a summary of all settings. Check participant count, question count, deadlines, and review types. You can save as **Draft** to return later or click **Launch** to activate the cycle.

<Callout type="warning">
Launching a cycle is not reversible. Once launched, Nami begins sending DMs to participants. Double-check all settings, especially deadlines and participant lists, before clicking Launch.
</Callout>

## Draft vs. Active Status

- **Draft** -- the cycle is saved but invisible to participants. Edit freely.
- **Active** -- the cycle is live. Nami is sending and collecting reviews. Limited edits are available (e.g., extending deadlines).

After all reviews are submitted and deadlines pass, the cycle moves to calibration (if enabled) and eventually to **Closed** status, at which point results are shared with employees.
