# My Team Dashboard

<RoleTag role="manager" />

The My Team page gives managers a single view of their direct reports' performance status, review progress, and goal activity.

## What You See

When you open **Dashboard > My Team**, each direct report is displayed as a card showing:

- **Pending reviews** -- the number of reviews waiting for your input.
- **Completed reviews** -- how many reviews you have already submitted for this person.
- **Latest rating** -- the most recent overall rating (1-5) from a completed cycle.
- **Active goals** -- count of goals currently in progress.
- **Avg goal progress** -- the mean completion percentage across all active goals.

<Callout type="tip">
Click any team member's card to jump directly to their full profile, review history, or goal details.
</Callout>

## Using the Dashboard Effectively

The dashboard is designed for at-a-glance prioritization. Focus on:

1. **High pending counts** -- these reviews need your attention before deadlines hit.
2. **Low goal progress** -- a team member falling behind may need a check-in.
3. **Rating trends** -- compare latest ratings against previous cycles to spot growth or regression.

## Filtering and Sorting

You can sort the team list by name, pending review count, or average goal progress. If you manage a large team, use the search bar to find a specific report quickly.

<Callout type="info">
The My Team view only shows your direct reports. If you need visibility into skip-level reports, ask your admin to grant extended access or use the Analytics module.
</Callout>

## Taking Action

From the dashboard you can start a review, view someone's competency profile, or navigate to their goals page -- all without leaving the My Team section.
