# Running Calibration Sessions

<RoleTag role="hr">HR</RoleTag> <RoleTag role="admin">Admin</RoleTag>

Calibration is the process of standardizing performance grades across your organization before releasing them to employees. It ensures that ratings mean the same thing regardless of which manager assigned them.

## Accessing Calibration

1. Navigate to **Dashboard > Cycles** and select the cycle.
2. Click the **Calibration** tab (available once cycle status is **Completed**).

<Callout type="info">
Department heads can calibrate within their own department. HR and admin users can calibrate across the entire organization.
</Callout>

## Grid View

The default view displays all review assignments. Each row shows the employee name, department, level, manager, overall rating (average of all reviewer scores), and a **final grade** dropdown.

Use the department filter to focus on one team. Assignments are grouped by department and can be expanded or collapsed.

### Grade Options

| Grade | Label |
|---|---|
| 5 | Exceptional |
| 4 | Exceeds Expectations |
| 3 | Meets Expectations |
| 2 | Below Expectations |
| 1 | Needs Improvement |

## Competency Heatmap

Switch to the **Heatmap** tab for a color-coded view of ratings across competencies. Pivot by different dimensions:

- **By Role** — compare across job functions
- **By Department** — spot team-level trends
- **By Level** — see how performance distributes across seniority
- **By Tenure** — compare newer vs. longer-tenured employees

<Callout type="tip">
If an entire department scores low on one competency, that signals a training need rather than individual performance issues.
</Callout>

## Adjusting Grades and Flagging Outliers

Select a new grade from the dropdown next to any employee, then click **Save**. Each adjustment is tracked with the calibrator's name and timestamp.

Watch for outliers: ratings that diverge significantly from peers at the same level, large gaps between self-assessment and manager rating, or incomplete submissions that distort averages.

<Callout type="warning">
Always investigate outliers before adjusting. A high or low score may be legitimate and supported by detailed reviewer comments.
</Callout>

## Releasing Grades

Once calibration is complete, click **Release Grades** on the cycle page. Employees can then see final grades on their Reviews and Feedback pages. This is a one-way action — the cycle status moves to **Closed**.
