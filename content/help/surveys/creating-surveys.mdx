# Creating & Running Surveys

<RoleTag role="hr" /> <RoleTag role="admin" />

Perf supports three survey types, each designed for a different listening purpose. Surveys are created in the web dashboard and delivered to participants through Nami in Slack.

## Survey Types

| Type | Purpose | Typical Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| **360-Degree** | Gather multi-rater feedback on an individual from peers, managers, and direct reports | Per cycle or on-demand |
| **Pulse** | Quick check-in on engagement, morale, or a specific topic | Weekly or monthly |
| **eNPS** | Measure employee loyalty with a single 0-10 question | Quarterly |

## Creating a Survey

1. Go to **Dashboard > Surveys > New Survey**.
2. Select the survey type.
3. Add your questions. Pulse surveys support **rating scales (1-7)** and **open text** questions. eNPS uses the standard promoter question (0-10) automatically.
4. Choose **participants** -- select individuals, departments, or the entire workspace.
5. Set a launch date and deadline.
6. Click **Launch**.

<Callout type="info">
Once launched, Nami sends each participant a Slack DM with a link to complete the survey. Reminders follow the same 7/3/1-day schedule as review deadlines.
</Callout>

## Viewing Results

Navigate to the survey detail page to see:

- **Response rate** -- how many participants have completed the survey.
- **Aggregate scores** -- averages, distributions, and trends per question.
- **Individual responses** -- available only to admins and HR, respecting any anonymity settings.

<Callout type="warning">
Anonymous surveys cannot be de-anonymized after launch. Choose the anonymity setting carefully before launching.
</Callout>

## Tips for High Response Rates

- Keep pulse surveys under 5 questions.
- Launch on Tuesday or Wednesday mornings for best completion.
- Use Nami reminders rather than manual follow-ups.
