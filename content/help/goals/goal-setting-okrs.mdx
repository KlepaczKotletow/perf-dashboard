# Setting & Tracking Goals

<RoleTag role="all" />

Nami helps you set, cascade, and track goals at every level of your organization. Whether you are an individual contributor aligning to team objectives or a leader defining company-wide priorities, the Goals module keeps everything connected.

## Creating a Goal

1. Navigate to **Dashboard > Goals > New Goal**.
2. Enter a title and optional description.
3. Choose the **scope**: Individual, Team, or Company.
4. Set **metrics** with a start value, current value, and target value so progress is calculated automatically.
5. Optionally link the goal to an active **review cycle** so it appears during self and manager assessments.

<Callout type="tip">
Use specific, measurable targets. Instead of "Improve retention," try "Reduce monthly churn from 5% to 3% by Q3."
</Callout>

## Goal Scopes

| Scope | Visible to | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Individual | Owner + manager | Personal development, role-specific KPIs |
| Team | All team members + leadership | Shared deliverables, squad OKRs |
| Company | Entire workspace | Strategic priorities, north-star metrics |

## Tracking Progress

Each goal displays a progress bar derived from your metric values. Update the **current value** at any time; the percentage recalculates instantly. Goals can have one of four tracking statuses: **On Track**, **At Risk**, **Delayed**, or **Achieved**.

## Goal Cascading

Company goals can be linked as parents of team goals, and team goals as parents of individual goals. This creates a cascade tree so leadership can see how top-level objectives break down across the organization.

<Callout type="info">
Goals linked to a cycle will surface in the Goal Setting phase, giving reviewers full context during assessments.
</Callout>

## Linking Goals to Cycles

When creating or editing a goal, select an active cycle from the dropdown. The goal then appears in the review form for that cycle, allowing self-assessors and managers to reference progress directly.
